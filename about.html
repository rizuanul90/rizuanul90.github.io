Who I Am

I'm an analytical IT professional with 4+ years of experience in high-stakes, regulated environments — from federal healthcare systems at the Veterans Affairs to global e-commerce platforms at The Estée Lauder Companies.

My background in quality assurance gave me a unique lens: I've always approached systems by asking "how could this break?" — which maps directly to the attacker's mindset at the core of cybersecurity.

Now I'm channeling that experience into a full transition into security. I'm currently completing the NPower Advanced Cybersecurity Cohort, building hands-on SOC skills, and working toward my bachelor's at John Jay College of Criminal Justice.

I'm passionate about threat detection, access control, incident response, and building tools that keep sensitive systems safe. Every project in this portfolio reflects a real skill — not just a tutorial I followed.
